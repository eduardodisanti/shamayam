  Largest deviation across the grid: 1.1 standard errors. The
  proposition is therefore verified as an EQUALITY, not only as the
  inequality coverage >= p, and the verification holds at the
  non-trivial values taken inside the degenerate range.

  'coverage' is P(D_new <= R_hat), estimated on a FRESH evaluation
  block of 300 windows per repetition, disjoint from the
  calibration draw. The certification requires it to be at least p=0.98;
  'bias %' is the relative departure of the mean certified radius from R_p.

  READ THE BIAS COLUMN, NOT ONLY THE MEAN. Inside the degenerate range
  the certified radius IS the sample maximum, so it drifts UPWARD with
  n (+2% at n=49 to +6% at n=98) and then falls discontinuously at
  n=99, where an interior order statistic first becomes available. A
  saturation curve computed this way cannot flatten inside the band:
  what looks like settling is the maximum of a growing sample.

  The interpolated estimator does produce the familiar smoothly
  saturating curve, approaching R_p from below - and it undercovers at
  every n (int.cov < p throughout). The reassuring visual and the
  coverage guarantee are therefore in tension: the estimator that makes
  the curve look convergent is precisely the one that fails to certify.

  plug-in versus split-sample reference
       n      split    plug-in   shift %  split cov   plug cov
      49   22.89290   22.37942     -2.24    0.97657    0.97899

     300   22.66845   22.15082     -2.28    0.97960    0.98010
     800   22.60264   21.98736     -2.72    0.98141    0.98061
  The predicted shrinkage is confirmed: a sample lies closer to its own
  centre and its own principal subspace than a fresh observation does,
  so the plug-in radius is 2-3% smaller. Coverage, however, is NOT
  degraded, because the evaluation distances are recomputed in the same
  plug-in geometry and the two shift together. The practical cost of a
  plug-in reference is therefore comparability of radii across assets
  and regimes, not loss of coverage at these sample sizes.

  radius by regime, under the shared nominal projection
  regime           R_p (certified)   ratio to nominal
  healthy                 23.12680               1.00
  outer_race              22.62539               0.98
  inner_race              22.77563               0.98
  ball_fault              23.94574               1.04

  NEGATIVE RESULT, reported as such. The fault regimes do NOT occupy
  materially larger radii in this projection: the ratios are 0.98-1.04,
  i.e. no separation. A 2-D principal projection of the raw window is
  dominated by the shaft harmonics, whereas the fault signature is a
  low-amplitude impulse train that contributes almost nothing to the
  leading variance directions.

  This matters for how saturation evidence should be read. The same
  simulator and the same nominal-only fitting detect these regimes in
  Experiment 5, where membership is tested through the reconstruction
  residual rather than a low-dimensional radius. The saturation radius
  is therefore a diagnostic of the NOMINAL geometry and its sampling,
  not a detector, and a saturation plot is not evidence of regime
  separability. It is also why the framework poses membership through a
  residual rather than through a radius in a projection.

EXPERIMENT 8 -- attractor drift under slow parameter change
==========================================================================
Chronological records of 700 windows, the first half nominal.
Severity then ramps smoothly. The radius is frozen after
commissioning and never adapted, so a persistent departure indicates
that the orbit has moved away from the commissioned neighbourhood.

  regime               tau  onset detected  delay  sev@det  FAR pre exceed post
  outer_race      0.004014    351      496    145    0.251     5.2%       96.1%
  inner_race      0.004014    351      473    122    0.211     5.2%       98.2%
  ball_fault      0.004014    351      545    194    0.335     5.2%       96.8%

  'onset' is the first record with non-zero severity, 'detected' the
  first persistent departure, and 'delay' the gap between them, all in
  record index. 'sev@det' is the severity actually reached at
  detection, so a small value means the departure was caught early in
  the ramp. 'FAR pre' is measured on post-commissioning records before
  onset, where the process is still nominal, and should sit near the
  target level of 2%.

  The detector is given no severity information, no fault labels and
  no adaptation after commissioning. What it reports is failure of
  membership in a region estimated before any degradation occurred.


EXPERIMENT 9 -- operator adequacy under the distance-proxy probe
==========================================================================
Latent codes are pushed away from the nominal range, decoded, and the
residual is compared against the true distance to a disjoint nominal
cloud. A residual that tracks distance rises; one that measures
distance to a larger structure containing the nominal set stays flat.

  The linear control accepts points hundreds of times the nominal
  spacing with residuals of order 1e-30: its accepted region is a slab
  around the retained subspace, not a tube around the nominal manifold.
  The convolutional operator accepts only at the nominal scale. Both
  attain the calibrated coverage, which is precisely why coverage
  cannot be used to choose between them.
